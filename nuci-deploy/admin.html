Reset statistics 

      
Starts the dashboard from a clean slate: cumulative counters (users, purchases, revenue, completed plans) count from the moment of the reset, and day charts start on that date. Nothing is deleted from Supabase - the reset only sets a marker, and "Show all-time" brings the full history back at any point. Current-state numbers (active plans, sessions today, credits) always show the real present state. The funnel keeps its own 7/30-day window, so it fully refreshes on its own within that window.

Erase all statistics

      
Permanently deletes the tracked events, funnel history and client errors from Supabase. Unlike the reset above, this cannot be undone and there is no "show all-time" afterwards. Accounts, plans, purchases and revenue are not touched - only the analytics.
